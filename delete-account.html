Delete your Studypath account

You can do this here even if you no longer have the app installed.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email, name, password hash) and profile photo.
  • All sign-in sessions on every device.
  • Any problem reports you sent while signed in.
  • All quiz history saved to your account, including questions, answers, scores and per-question records. This is removed by a background job within minutes.

Deleting is permanent and cannot be undone. Data kept only in your browser on a device (for example, local quiz history) stays there until you clear your browser data or use Settings → Clear all local data in the app. Our providers may keep routine logs and backups for a limited time, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Delete now

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Or enter your account email and password below. Signed in with Google and never set a Studypath password? Delete from Settings while signed in instead, leaving the password blank, because there is no Studypath password for that account.

Using Studypath as a guest?

Guests have no account, so there is nothing to sign in to. Quiz results saved under a random device ID are not linked to your name or email. Use Settings → Clear all local data to remove what is on your device, or contact us if you want help with anything else.
